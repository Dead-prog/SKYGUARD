# SKYGUARD
My project is to develop a swarm of VTOL drones capable of searching large areas and helping rescue teams find people in danger. Each drone will send live video and its position to a central control station, giving rescuers a better view of the situation.

My project has three main advantages compared with helicopters.

First, cost. Deploying several helicopters is very expensive, while VTOL drones could cover large areas at a lower cost.

Second, pilot efficiency. Each helicopter requires a pilot, but my system aims to allow one experienced operator to supervise several VTOL drones at the same time. In the future, AI could help the operator manage the drones more efficiently. However, AI is not part of the first phase of my project.

Third, observation. Cameras can provide a different perspective and help rescuers identify details that might be difficult to notice from the ground.

I chose VTOL aircraft because I want to combine the advantages of vertical take-off and landing with the efficiency of fixed-wing flight. Unlike a conventional quadcopter, a fixed-wing VTOL can generate lift using its wings during forward flight instead of relying entirely on its propellers. This could help reduce energy consumption and increase flight time, allowing the drones to search larger areas.

# SKYGUARD: From a Dream to Reality

For now, SKYGUARD is a dream, but I am determined to turn it into reality. To achieve this, I have developed a plan divided into three phases. Each phase will allow me to acquire new skills and build the knowledge needed for the next step.

### Phase 1: Quadcopter Development

First, I will build a small quadcopter powered by an ESP32. This will help me learn the fundamentals of electronics, programming, flight control, and aircraft design. It will also allow me to test the propulsion system and develop my own control interface.

### Phase 2: First VTOL Prototype

Next, I will develop my first VTOL aircraft, capable of vertical takeoff and landing, transitioning to horizontal flight, and flying forward efficiently. I will integrate GPS, a camera, and other sensors to collect useful information for search and rescue missions. This phase will also help me explore communication between the aircraft and a ground control station.

### Phase 3: Multi-VTOL Coordination

Finally, I will build two additional VTOL aircraft and work on coordinating all three. I will focus on communication between the aircraft, mission coordination, and sharing information with the ground control station.

### Final Goal

The long-term goal of SKYGUARD is to develop an affordable aerial search and rescue system capable of covering large areas and helping rescuers locate people in emergency situations. By progressing step by step, I aim to turn this ambitious idea into a functional prototype while developing the technical skills needed to improve it over time.
