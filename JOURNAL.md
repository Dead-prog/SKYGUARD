Phase 1: First drone prototype

Worked on controlling the four motors of a small quadcopter using an ESP32-S3. Tested motor control and started reading pitch and roll data from the MPU6050 for the future PID controller.

Status: In progress

Phase 2: First VTOL drone

The goal is to build the first VTOL drone equipped with GPS, a standard camera and a thermal camera to help rescue teams locate people in danger.

Status: Planned

Phase 3: VTOL swarm

The goal is to build two additional VTOL drones and enable communication between the three drones to improve search and rescue operations.

Status: Planned
